## Full description (4000)

"What do you want for dinner?" "I don't know, you choose." "No, you choose."

Swipe for Dinner ends that loop. Each of you swipes through dinner recipes on your own phone, in private. Swipe right on what you'd happily eat tonight, left on what you wouldn't. Your partner never sees what you passed on. At the time you choose, the app shows you the dishes you both said yes to.

Swipe alone. Eat together.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pair up with your partner using a short invite code.
• Each of you sets food preferences once: diet, allergies, ingredients you dislike and the cuisines you love. Dishes that don't fit either of you never show up.
• Swipe whenever you like. You both see the same dishes, at your own pace.
• At your reveal time, see your match: the dishes you both liked. No match? You'll see the closest ones, or our best pick, and you can swipe one more round.

COOK IT AT HOME
• Every dish comes with the full recipe: ingredients and step-by-step instructions, and a video when there is one.
• Tick off what you already have, then share the shopping list with whatever's missing.
• Past dinners are kept in the Dinners tab, so a dish you loved is never lost.

MADE FOR TWO
• One subscription covers both of you.
• A daily reminder at your reveal time, if you want one.
• Subscribers can share their own recipes with every couple on Swipe for Dinner after a quick review.

FREE TRIAL, THEN A SIMPLE PLAN
Your 14-day free trial starts when your partner joins. After that, one of you subscribes monthly or yearly to keep going, and it covers both of you. Your preferences and history are kept if you pause.

No ads, and we don't sell your data.

Allergen information is worked out from each recipe's ingredients. Always check the ingredients yourself if you have an allergy.
